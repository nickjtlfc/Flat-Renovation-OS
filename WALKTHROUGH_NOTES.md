## Demolition intentions

- Room C is to be modelled primarily as its intended post-demolition shell.
- The current small-bedroom/internal partitioning is intended to be removed.
- A wall omitted from the hand plan is also said to be scheduled for removal.
- C1 and C2 retention/removal is not fully established.
- The description “thin internal wall” is not a structural assessment.
- No demolition shown or discussed here should be considered technically or legally approved.

## Proposed renovation ideas

- Divide the post-demolition Room C shell into a kitchen and bedroom with a better-positioned new partition.
- Potentially remove D5, move the WC forward and use the rear area for an unspecified boiler-type appliance.
- Use the Room B stair-soffit area as part of the future shower arrangement.
- Add a serving hatch between Rooms A and C, as recorded elsewhere in the project.

All are conceptual. The serving hatch is especially structurally sensitive because the plan labels the A–C wall structural.

## Uncertainties and transcription issues

1. “Proper version” of the plan at 00:01–00:05 refers to a future source that is not present.
2. “That's background to D one” at 00:44–00:46 is unclear and should not be silently corrected.
3. The wording at 02:10–02:16 is garbled; its probable meaning is that modelling Room C as one open room may otherwise be confusing.
4. “Small in in a bedroom” at 02:24–02:30 is probably a transcription error referring to the small current bedroom/internal enclosure.
5. “One big square room” is qualitative; it must not be used to force Room C into square geometry.
6. C2 reaches “a certain height,” but no height is stated.
7. “Back wall” and “long back wall” are camera-relative descriptions, not confirmed compass-wall names.
8. “Some sort of water boiler” is technically imprecise and describes only current thinking.
9. The wall at 02:59 and the wall at 03:42 may be parts of the same partition or separate removable elements.
10. Door, window and cupboard IDs are transcript-supported but still require an annotated plan review before they are treated as geometrically verified.
