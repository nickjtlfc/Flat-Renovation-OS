## 1. Survey conventions

- Existing finished floor level is the floor/height datum.
- Supplemental horizontal measurements were taken at approximately **1350 mm above finished floor level (AFFL)** unless stated otherwise.
- Blu Tack nodes represent points on the **finished wall plane**.
- Where door or window casing projects in front of the wall, the wall-plane node remains behind that projection.
- Decorative casing-face dimensions and casing projections are separate from wall-plane node positions.
- Raw readings are preserved even where repeat measurements differ.
- A missing or blocked reading must not be inferred unless Codex derives it from other stated constraints and labels it as derived.
- Photographs and this typed evidence pack should be used together where handwritten source sheets are ambiguous.

## 9. Evidence hierarchy for Codex

Use evidence in this order:

1. Baseline wall-chain measurements and exact wall-plane nodes.
2. Supplemental node-to-node triangulation.
3. Supplemental node-to-wall and wall-to-wall constraints.
4. Door and window object measurements.
5. Photographs for interpreting casing layers, projections and endpoint meaning.

Where evidence conflicts:

- retain every raw reading;
- report the residual or mismatch;
- do not silently average;
- identify the smallest set of readings that cannot coexist geometrically;
- prefer a provisional best-fit reconstruction over rejecting the entire room.

## 10. Instructions for first-pass reconstruction

The first reconstruction should include:

- Room A finished-wall shell;
- node labels A0-A7;
- opening/node positions for W1, D1 and D2;
- a separate casing/front-face layer for W1, D1 and D2;
- measurement residuals or contradictions;
- a simple 2D SVG or equivalent visual.

The first reconstruction should **not** attempt to finalise:

- structural wall cores;
- concealed construction;
- decorative moulding profiles;
- perfect 3D ceiling surfaces;

## 11. Explicit cautions

- **BASE-A-07 is a direct field measurement of 1431 mm from W1-AR to A6.** It is authoritative baseline evidence and must not be replaced by a casing-face dimension or treated as an inferred span.
- **SUP-061 is lower-confidence as a geometric constraint** because neither endpoint is an exact node. It can be excluded from the first solver pass.
- The handwritten source sheets reused some row headings for measurements that were physically different. The typed meanings in this document take precedence for interpretation, while the numeric readings remain raw evidence.
- D1 and W1 front-face widths are not equivalent by definition to the associated wall-plane node spans.
