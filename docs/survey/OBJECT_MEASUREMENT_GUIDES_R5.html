Generic door-with-casing guide

Apply to D1-A, D2-A/C, D3-B/C, D4-C and D5-B/WC. Diagram is not to scale.
R5 OBJECT GUIDE 1 / 3

Positioning rule

The approved CL/CR nodes are the outer visible casing edges. Measure adjoining wall segments to those nodes. The door leaf is never the positioning datum.

Diagram key

D01-D02: clear opening
D03-D04: outer casing
D05-D08: trims, lining depth, projection
D09-D10: ceiling and floor verticals

Measured faces

D1: Room A only. D4: Room C only.

D2: A and C. D3: B and C. D5: B and WC.

Left/right always means your left/right while standing in the named area facing the door.

Field note

D10 may be zero where the selected lower casing/frame datum meets FFL. Record the actual datum and zero; do not silently omit it.

D07 is finished lining/wall depth, not hidden structural thickness.

Generic window-with-casing guide

Apply to W1 from Room A and W2 from Room C. Diagram is not to scale.
R5 OBJECT GUIDE 2 / 3

Approved positioning nodes

W1: W1-AL and W1-AR from Room A.

W2: W2-CL and W2-CR from Room C.

These casing/opening edges replace generic old Room C nodes.

Diagram key

W01-W02: outer visible boundary
W03-W04: visible frame
W05: accessible recess depth
W06-W08: ceiling and floor verticals

Positioning examples

W1: A5 to W1-AL, then W1-AR to A6.

W2: C2 to W2-CL and W2-CR to C1 in viewer-left/right language.

Stand in the named room facing the window before assigning left/right.

Field note

Measure both W07 and W08 if the sill and lower outer opening/casing datum differ. Photograph the chosen boundaries.

Do not infer concealed masonry openings behind plaster, trim or frame.

Generic cupboard footprint and vertical guide

Apply to CP1 and CP2 using approved CP casing/footprint nodes. Diagram is not to scale.
R5 OBJECT GUIDE 3 / 3

Approved footprint nodes

CP1: CP1-CL, CP1-CR, CP1-FL, CP1-FR.

CP2: CP2-CL, CP2-CR, CP2-FL, CP2-FR.

CL/CR are wall-side casing corners; FL/FR are front footprint corners.

Diagram key

C01-C02/C05: exterior footprint and height
C03-C04: left/right projection
C06-C08: accessible internal clear space
C09-C10: ceiling and floor verticals

Essential 3D verticals

C09: upper casing/top to ceiling.

C10: bottom/base/lower casing to finished floor.

Record zero where a nominated base actually meets FFL. Name and photograph the chosen lower reference.

Do not infer

Internal shelves, cupboard doors, stored items and boiler/cylinder components are not positioning datums.

Mark inaccessible internal readings blocked. Never substitute an internal dimension for the external footprint.
