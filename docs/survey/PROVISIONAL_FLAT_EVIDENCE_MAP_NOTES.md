## Status and topology authority

`PROVISIONAL_FLAT_EVIDENCE_MAP.svg` has passed the topology review gate only for use as the conceptual basis of survey design. It is not a dimensioned floor plan, accepted shell or geometry source of truth.

The principal topology evidence is now:

1. `source-material/plans/rough-paint-sketch.jpg`, as amended by the user to label Room B correctly; and
2. `source-material/plans/2dPlan.jpeg`.

The rough sketch is authoritative for the general shell arrangement, footprint shape, room relationships, major recesses/projections and purple removable bedroom enclosure. The original plan refines identifiable walls, openings, cupboard relationships and fixed features. Neither source establishes exact proportions, angles, wall thicknesses or survey-grade dimensions.

All room photographs were rechecked as a contact review. The walkthrough evidence was compared through the existing sampled-frame review, full SRT and `WALKTHROUGH_NOTES.md`. These sources support identities and circulation but do not replace the two plan sources for overhead topology.

## 1. How the previous SVG misunderstood the flat

The superseded SVG imposed three large block-like room shapes. That caused several material errors:

1. Room B was drawn as a broad block beneath the A–C junction instead of a narrower downward projection.
2. The WC was drawn as a compartment within that broad block rather than an attached upper-right part of the Room B/WC footprint.
3. The stepped upper footprint around C1 and C2 was lost.
4. The lower Room C W2 recess and the sequence of lower-edge notches/openings were simplified incorrectly.
5. The current Room C bedroom enclosure was drawn as a freestanding central/bottom box. The new sketch shows two purple partition lines which, with permanent walls, enclose the upper-right portion of Room C.
6. D2, D3, D4 and D1 were not represented in the correct general relationship along the structural wall and lower shell edges.
7. The drawing’s large rectangles made uncertain proportions appear more regular than the evidence allowed.

The previous SVG is therefore retained only through this written correction record; it is no longer topology authority.

## 5. Remaining uncertainty and unresolved conflicts

1. Every drawn length, angle and relative proportion remains unverified.
2. The precise short wall returns around D4 and D3 remain difficult to reconcile from the rough sketch and angled original plan, except that the physical survey now confirms the turn at `D3-CL` and the 580 mm adjoining-wall run to `D2-CR`.
3. Door-leaf lines in the rough sketch suggest general swing directions, but hinge sides and swings have not been verified for every door.
4. D2 clearly enters permanent Room C, and `D2-CR` is fixed locally at the observed 580 mm separation from `D3-CL`; its broader position, opposite jamb/width, swing and wall bearing remain provisional.
5. The exact D5 opening position within the B–WC boundary remains approximate.
6. The Room A boundary is now continuous above Room B and the WC, but its exact length, wall build-up and junction positions need survey confirmation.
7. C1/C2 are clearly labelled, but their precise relationship to the external shell, internal cupboard fronts and retained services is not measured.
8. The latest sketch establishes the chimney breast’s general stepped shape, not its real width, depth, angles or position.
9. The gold soffit box establishes a general location, not its real width, slope direction or section.
10. The proposed A–C serving hatch has no accepted position or size and is therefore mentioned but not drawn as an opening.
11. The Room C purple enclosure may represent two connected partition faces, but wall thickness, junction construction and whether any related return is separate remain unknown.
12. The C1 service symbol is positioned from user clarification, but the actual inlet/equipment point and routes remain unmeasured.
13. The electrical entry is positioned immediately right of D1 from user clarification, but its exact point and route remain unmeasured.
14. The full shape of common circulation outside D1 and the small shared area outside D4 is missing.
15. No remaining material room-identity conflict is known after the user amended the lower sketch label to `ROOM B`.
